A bright, creamy, all-purpose dressing for grain bowls, roasted vegetables, and salads.

## Ingredients

- 1/3 cup tahini, well stirred
- 3 tbsp fresh lemon juice (about 1 lemon)
- 1 small garlic clove, finely grated
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
- 1/4 to 1/3 cup cold water
- 1 tsp maple syrup or honey (optional)

## Steps

1. Add the tahini, lemon juice, garlic, and salt to a bowl or jar.
2. Whisk until combined; it will seize up and get thick. That's normal.
3. Whisk in cold water 1 tablespoon at a time until smooth and pourable.
4. Taste and adjust with more salt, lemon, or the sweetener.

## Notes & Variations

- Keeps 1 week in the fridge. It thickens when cold; loosen with a splash of water.
- Herby: blend in a handful of parsley or cilantro.
- Spicy: add a pinch of cumin and a spoonful of harissa.
