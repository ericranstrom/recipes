Crispy roasted chickpeas over herby quinoa with crunchy vegetables and a generous pour of lemon-tahini dressing.

## Ingredients

### Chickpeas

- 2 cans (15 oz each) chickpeas, drained, rinsed, and patted dry
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt

### Grain

- 1 cup quinoa, rinsed
- 1 3/4 cups water
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 1/4 cup chopped parsley

### Bowls

- 1 English cucumber, diced
- 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1/2 cup crumbled feta (omit for vegan)
- 1/4 cup Kalamata olives, halved
- 3/4 cup [Lemon-Tahini Dressing](../../components/lemon-tahini-dressing.md)

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 425°F.
2. Toss the chickpeas with the olive oil, paprika, cumin, and salt on a sheet pan.
3. Roast the chickpeas for 25 to 30 minutes, shaking the pan halfway, until crisp.
4. Meanwhile, bring the quinoa, water, and salt to a boil in a small pot.
5. Cover, reduce to low, and simmer for 15 minutes.
6. Take the quinoa off the heat and let it steam, covered, for 5 minutes.
7. Fluff the quinoa with a fork and stir in the parsley.
8. Make the [Lemon-Tahini Dressing](../../components/lemon-tahini-dressing.md).
9. Divide the quinoa among bowls and top with the chickpeas, cucumber, tomatoes, onion, feta, and olives.
10. Drizzle each bowl with dressing.

## Notes & Variations

- Make-ahead: store the quinoa, chickpeas, chopped veg, and dressing separately for up to 4 days. Chickpeas soften in the fridge; re-crisp in a hot oven for 5 minutes.
- Swap the quinoa for farro or brown rice (adjust cook time to the package).
- Add roasted sweet potato or a jammy egg for a heartier bowl.
