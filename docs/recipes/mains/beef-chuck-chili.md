Rich, smoky chili of beef chuck braised with bacon, red wine, and chipotle until tender, finished with beans the next day.

## Ingredients

### Meat and vegetables

- 2 1/2 lb beef chuck, cut into 1-inch cubes
- 1 lb bacon, chopped into 1/2-inch pieces
- 2 onions, diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 1 poblano pepper, diced
- 5 to 6 garlic cloves, minced

### Liquids and tomato

- 28 oz crushed tomatoes
- 2 cups beef stock, plus more as needed
- 1/2 cup dry red wine
- 2 tbsp tomato paste

### Spices

- 2 tbsp ancho chili powder
- 1 tbsp regular chili powder
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp Mexican oregano
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp black pepper

### To finish

- 2 chipotle peppers in adobo, minced
- 1 to 2 tsp adobo sauce
- 2 bay leaves
- 3 cans (15 oz each) beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- Kosher salt

## Steps

### Day 1: Brown and braise

1. Heat the oven to 300°F.
2. Put the bacon in a cold Dutch oven and set it over medium heat.
3. Cook, stirring now and then, until the bacon is browned and the fat has rendered.
4. Scoop out the bacon with a slotted spoon and set it aside.
5. Pour off all but 2 to 3 tbsp of the bacon fat.
6. Pat the beef dry and season it generously with salt and pepper.
7. Raise the heat to medium-high and brown the beef in batches, 1 to 2 minutes per side. It doesn't need to cook through.
8. Move the browned beef to a plate.
9. Lower the heat to medium and add the onions.
10. Cook the onions 5 to 8 minutes, until soft and starting to brown.
11. Add the bell pepper and poblano and cook 4 to 5 minutes more.
12. Add the garlic and cook about 30 seconds.
13. Stir in the tomato paste and cook 2 to 3 minutes, until it darkens a bit.
14. Add all the spices and stir constantly for 30 to 45 seconds, until fragrant.
15. Pour in the red wine and scrape up the browned bits from the bottom of the pot.
16. Simmer 3 to 5 minutes to reduce the wine slightly.
17. Add the crushed tomatoes, beef stock, chipotles, adobo sauce, and bay leaves.
18. Return the beef and bacon to the pot, stir, and bring to a gentle simmer.
19. Cover and move the pot to the oven.
20. Braise 2 1/2 to 3 hours, checking now and then, until the beef is fork-tender and starting to fall apart.
21. Take the pot out of the oven.
22. Cool the chili quickly in shallow containers or an ice bath.
23. Refrigerate overnight. It will thicken a lot as it chills.

### Day 2: Beans and finish

24. Return the chili to the Dutch oven and reheat gently over medium-low, stirring occasionally.
25. Stir in the beans.
26. Simmer gently 20 to 30 minutes so the beans heat through and pick up flavor.
27. Loosen with more stock or water if it's too thick.
28. Remove the bay leaves.
29. Stir in the vinegar.
30. Taste and adjust the salt, heat, and thickness.
31. Rest 5 to 10 minutes, then serve hot.

## Notes & Variations

- Making it a day ahead gives the best flavor. To serve the same day, skip the chill: add the beans right after the braise and pick up at step 25.
- More heat: add a third chipotle or more adobo sauce.
- Yield is about 6 to 8 generous bowls, or 12 to 16 smaller portions for a potluck.
