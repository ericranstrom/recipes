A holiday family tradition: baked macaroni in a creamy sharp cheddar and Gruyère sauce under a crisp, buttery panko topping.

## Ingredients

### Topping

- 2 tbsp unsalted butter
- 1 1/2 cups panko

### Cheese sauce

- 6 tbsp unsalted butter, plus more for the dish
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 5 1/2 cups milk
- 2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp freshly grated nutmeg
- 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper, or to taste

### Cheese

- 4 1/2 cups grated sharp white cheddar (about 18 oz): 3 cups for the sauce, 1 1/2 cups for the top
- 2 cups grated Gruyère (about 8 oz): 1 1/2 cups for the sauce, 1/2 cup for the top
    - Or swap in 1 1/4 cups grated Pecorino Romano (about 5 oz): 1 cup for the sauce, 1/4 cup for the top

### Pasta

- 1 lb elbow macaroni

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 375°F.
2. Butter a 3-quart baking dish.
3. Melt 2 tbsp butter in a small saucepan over medium heat.
4. Toss the panko with the melted butter in a medium bowl and set aside.
5. Warm the milk in a medium saucepan over medium heat.
6. Melt the remaining 6 tbsp butter in a high-sided skillet over medium heat.
7. Once the butter bubbles, whisk in the flour and cook 1 minute, whisking.
8. Slowly pour in the hot milk, whisking the whole time.
9. Keep cooking and whisking until the sauce bubbles and thickens.
10. Take the pan off the heat.
11. Stir in the salt, nutmeg, black pepper, cayenne, 3 cups cheddar, and 1 1/2 cups Gruyère (or 1 cup Pecorino).
12. Bring a large pot of water to a boil.
13. Cook the macaroni 2 to 3 minutes less than the package directions, so the center is still underdone.
14. Drain the macaroni in a colander.
15. Rinse it under cold water and drain well.
16. Stir the macaroni into the cheese sauce.
17. Pour it into the buttered dish.
18. Sprinkle the remaining 1 1/2 cups cheddar and 1/2 cup Gruyère (or 1/4 cup Pecorino) over the top.
19. Scatter the buttered panko over the cheese.
20. Bake about 30 minutes, until browned on top.
21. Cool on a rack for 5 minutes, then serve hot.

## Notes & Variations

- Undercooking the pasta matters: it finishes cooking in the sauce in the oven. Brands vary, so check the package time.
- The original topping is 6 slices good white bread, crusts removed, torn into 1/4- to 1/2-inch pieces and tossed with the 2 tbsp melted butter.
