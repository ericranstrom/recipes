A simple, reliable cinnamon-scented banana bread with a tender crumb.

## Ingredients

### Creamed base

- 1 cup (200 g) granulated sugar
- 8 tbsp (1 stick, 113 g) unsalted butter, at room temperature, plus more for the pan
- 2 large eggs

### Banana mixture

- 3 ripe bananas
- 1 tbsp milk
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon

### Dry ingredients

- 2 cups (250 g) all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 325°F.
2. Butter a 9 x 5-inch loaf pan.
3. Beat the sugar and butter in a large bowl until pale and fluffy.
4. Beat in the eggs one at a time, mixing well after each.
5. In a small bowl, mash the bananas with a fork.
6. Stir the milk and cinnamon into the bananas.
7. In another bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
8. Stir the banana mixture into the butter mixture until combined.
9. Add the dry ingredients and mix only until no dry flour is visible.
10. Scrape the batter into the prepared pan.
11. Bake 60 to 70 minutes, until a toothpick in the center comes out clean.
12. Cool in the pan on a rack for 15 minutes.
13. Turn the loaf out of the pan onto the rack.
14. Cool completely before slicing.

## Notes & Variations

- The riper the bananas, the sweeter and more flavorful the bread. Heavily speckled or brown is ideal.
