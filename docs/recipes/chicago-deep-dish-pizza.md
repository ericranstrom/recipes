Tall, buttery, flaky-crusted deep dish with the cheese on the bottom and a thick, oregano-scented tomato sauce on top.

## Ingredients

### Dough

- 3 1/4 cups (406 g) all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup (60 g) yellow cornmeal
- 1 1/4 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp (12 g) granulated sugar
- 2 1/4 tsp (7 g) instant yeast (1 packet)
- 1 1/4 cups (300 ml) warm water, about 90°F
- 1/2 cup (113 g) unsalted butter, divided:
    - 1/4 cup (57 g) melted
    - 1/4 cup (57 g) softened to room temperature
- Olive oil, for the bowl and crust edges

### Tomato sauce

- 2 tbsp (28 g) unsalted butter
- 1 small onion, grated (about 1/3 cup)
- 3/4 tsp salt
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp crushed red pepper flakes (optional)
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1/4 tsp granulated sugar

### Toppings

- 4 cups (about 16 oz) shredded mozzarella
- 1/2 cup (45 g) grated Parmesan
- 1/2 cup sliced pepperoni (optional)
- 4 slices bacon, cooked and crumbled (optional)

## Steps

### Make the dough

1. In a large bowl or stand mixer with the dough hook, combine the flour, cornmeal, salt, sugar, and yeast.
2. Add the warm water and the melted butter.
3. Mix on low until everything is moistened.
4. Knead in the mixer or by hand for 4 to 5 minutes, until soft, smooth, and pulling away from the bowl. Add 1 tsp water if it's stiff, or 1 tbsp flour if it's sticky.
5. Shape the dough into a ball.
6. Oil a large bowl, add the dough, and turn it to coat.
7. Cover with foil and let rise somewhere warm for 1 to 2 hours, until doubled.

### Laminate the dough

8. Punch the dough down on a lightly floured surface.
9. Roll it into a 15 x 12-inch rectangle.
10. Spread the softened butter evenly over the dough.
11. Roll it up tightly, starting from a long side.
12. Cut the log in half.
13. Shape each half into a ball.
14. Return the balls to the oiled bowl, cover with foil, and refrigerate 1 hour.

### Make the sauce (while the dough chills)

15. Melt the butter in a medium saucepan over medium heat.
16. Add the onion, salt, oregano, and pepper flakes.
17. Cook about 5 minutes, until the onion is lightly browned.
18. Stir in the garlic, tomatoes, and sugar.
19. Lower the heat to medium-low and simmer about 30 minutes, until thick and reduced to about 2 1/2 cups.
20. Take the sauce off the heat and set aside.

### Assemble and bake

21. Heat the oven to 425°F.
22. Grease two 9 x 2-inch round cake pans or 9-inch springform pans.
23. Take one dough ball out of the fridge, leaving the other chilled.
24. Roll it into a 12-inch circle.
25. Press it into a pan, across the bottom and up the sides.
26. Trim off any excess dough at the rim.
27. Brush the exposed dough edges with olive oil.
28. Repeat with the second dough ball.
29. Spread 2 cups mozzarella over the bottom of each crust.
30. Add the pepperoni and bacon, if using.
31. Spoon about 1 1/4 cups sauce over each pizza (3/4 cup for a lighter sauce layer).
32. Sprinkle each with 1/4 cup Parmesan.
33. Set both pans on a large baking sheet to catch drips.
34. Bake 20 to 28 minutes, until the crust is golden. If the tops brown too fast, tent loosely with foil after 15 minutes.
35. Cool in the pans on a rack for 10 minutes.
36. Slice and serve.

## Notes & Variations

- Make-ahead dough: after the lamination and chill (step 14), refrigerate up to 24 hours, or freeze up to 2 months. Thaw frozen dough overnight in the fridge, then let it sit at room temperature 1 hour before rolling. Punch out any air bubbles before rolling.
- Make-ahead sauce: refrigerate up to 2 days or freeze up to 2 months.
- Leftovers keep up to 5 days in the fridge. Reheat in a 300°F oven for 15 to 20 minutes.
