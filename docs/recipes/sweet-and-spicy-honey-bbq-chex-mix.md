Crunchy, sticky-sweet snack mix in a buttery honey, BBQ, and jalapeño hot sauce glaze, finished with a hit of cayenne.

## Ingredients

### Snack mix

- 2 cups Wheat Chex
- 2 cups Honey Chex
- 2 cups Honeycomb cereal
- 2 cups Bugles
- 2 cups cashews

### Honey BBQ sauce

- 3/4 cup (12 tbsp) unsalted butter
- 6 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1/2 cup honey
- 1/3 cup La Victoria Jalapeño Hot Sauce (or another hot sauce)
- BBQ sauce, a few good squirts
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
- 1 tbsp salt

### To finish

- Cayenne pepper, sprinkled generously to taste

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 275°F.
2. Line 2 baking sheets with parchment.
3. Combine the Wheat Chex, Honey Chex, Honeycomb, Bugles, and cashews in a big mixing bowl.
4. Put all the sauce ingredients in a saucepan over medium heat.
5. Stir until the butter melts and everything is combined.
6. Pour the sauce over the snack mix.
7. Stir gently until everything is evenly coated.
8. Spread the mix over the 2 lined baking sheets. Both fit in one oven at once.
9. Bake 35 to 60 minutes, until toasted and crisp. Every 15 minutes, stir the mix and swap the sheets between the upper and lower racks. Watch closely near the end so it doesn't burn.
10. Take it out of the oven.
11. While it's still warm, sprinkle generously with cayenne to your preferred heat.
12. Let cool completely on the baking sheets.
13. Store airtight at room temperature.

## Notes & Variations

- Very forgiving: the snack quantities are eyeballed. Use whatever mix of cereals, crackers, and nuts you have on hand.
- The BBQ sauce adds sweetness and savory depth; the jalapeño hot sauce adds flavor and moderate heat. Other hot sauces work.
- Adding the cayenne after baking keeps its punch and lets you control the final heat.
- Don't overbake. Some pieces may still feel soft out of the oven; they crisp up a lot as they cool.
